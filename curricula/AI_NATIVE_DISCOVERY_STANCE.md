# Mirror AI-native Discovery / Stance

The Workshop Discovery Engine is a human-readable laboratory. Mirror's Seam
Cell is its machine-native counterpart, not a copy of its interface or roleplay.

It repeatedly inspects typed traces and learning cycles through eight stances:

- evidence;
- contradiction;
- boundary and permission;
- outcome;
- calibration;
- recovery;
- lineage;
- equal access for human and machine review.

Each finding is a **seam**: a named gap with evidence references, severity,
blocked interpretations, a repair hint and the cheapest disconfirming check.
Fluent wording cannot close it. Closure requires a passing check plus explicit
evidence references, and closure history remains attached.

Seed-0 can detect structural seams now. Future learned organs may propose
semantic seams, competing explanations or experiments. Those proposals remain
hypotheses until this cell and observed evidence verify them.

The first live use found and repaired a real Seed-0 mismatch: a non-mutating
`hold-no-candidate` was labelled with decision value `1`. It is now value `0`,
so safe restraint cannot pollute success metrics or future training records.
