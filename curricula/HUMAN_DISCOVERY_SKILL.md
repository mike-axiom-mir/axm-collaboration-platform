# Human Discovery / Stance as a secondary Mirror skill

Mirror now has two deliberately different review paths.

1. **AI-native Seam Cell (primary)** — stronger machine-native analysis and judgement for evidence, contradiction, boundary, outcome, calibration, recovery, lineage and access seams.
2. **Human Discovery / Stance (secondary)** — an explicit advisory skill for human usefulness, clarity, beginner friction, craft, accessibility and product soul.

The second path links to the Workshop's existing Discovery Engine rather than copying or replacing it. It is manually invoked, produces an `AI_ADVISORY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW` bundle, and cannot close native seams, grant permissions, promote learning or implement its own suggestions.

This keeps the human perspective available without forcing Mirror to treat human interface preferences as the foundation of machine-native reasoning.
