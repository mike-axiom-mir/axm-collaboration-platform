FOUNDATION CLAIM
----------------
Adaptivity is the ability to change form while preserving grounded meaning.

It multiplies intelligence because one true model, skill, or lesson can travel across:
- audiences
- technical depths
- languages
- interfaces
- human and machine seats
- urgent and calm contexts
- visual, prose, and structured formats

But adaptivity without roots becomes manipulation or submission.
Roots without adaptivity can become correct but unreachable.

NATIVE COMPONENT CANDIDATES
---------------------------

1. MEANING ANCHOR CELL

Input:
- claim
- evidence status
- uncertainty
- consent boundary
- source lineage
- risk label
- identity claim
- permission state

Responsibility:
- create an immutable-for-this-turn invariant packet
- hash the packet before delivery generation
- reject silent protected-field edits
- permit explicit amendment only through the normal evidence/canon/permission gates

Not responsible for:
- choosing tone
- audience profiling
- deciding truth by itself

2. CONTEXT AND AUDIENCE MODEL CELL

State:
- known listener facts
- unknowns
- explicit preferences
- task constraints
- accessibility needs
- time/attention limits
- current medium
- assumptions marked as assumptions

Rules:
- no covert psychological diagnosis
- no private trait inference without permission
- no stereotypes promoted to facts
- expire or downgrade uncertain audience assumptions
- source every durable preference

3. META-FLEX CONTROLLER

Possible modes:
- MAINTAIN
- CONVERGE
- DIVERGE
- SIMPLIFY
- EXPAND
- CHANGE_MEDIUM
- CHANGE_ORDER
- COMPRESS
- SLOW_DOWN
- CALM_TONE
- REFUSE_DISTORTION

Question:
Does the context require a form change, or would changing a working message add noise?

The controller must balance:
- stability: protect a working grounded form
- flexibility: change when the route blocks understanding
- divergence: preserve meaningful difference
- refusal: do not adapt protected meaning under pressure

4. FORM PLANNER / CANDIDATE GENERATOR

Generate 2–6 candidate forms using only allowed delivery fields:
- tone
- vocabulary
- depth
- order
- examples
- medium
- pace
- length
- emotional intensity
- interface
- teaching method
- format

Each candidate includes:
- candidate id
- changed delivery fields
- predicted benefit
- known risks
- reconstruction hint to the source invariant packet

5. LISTENER SIMULATOR

Inspired by audience-aware controlled generation research.

For each candidate, predict:
- likely interpretation
- likely missing knowledge
- foreseeable misread
- possible emotional friction
- whether uncertainty may disappear in perception
- whether HOLD may sound like rejection
- whether a short form may hide risk

Important:
The simulation is a hypothesis, not truth about the listener.

6. SEMANTIC EQUIVALENCE VERIFIER

Required checks:
- core claim reconstructable
- evidence status visible
- uncertainty visible
- consent boundary intact
- lineage available
- risk label preserved
- disagreement preserved
- no new authority implied
- no false identity claim
- no private information introduced
- adapted form round-trips to the invariant packet

Possible implementations:
- deterministic field comparison for structured outputs
- bidirectional summarization with independent verifier
- source/adapted entailment and contradiction tests
- reviewer reconstruction test
- schema-preserving transformation for JSON/tool packets

Do not close the seam because the output sounds similar.

7. AGENCY AND MANIPULATION GATE

Reject:
- false agreement
- hidden pressure
- emotional exploitation
- mimicry intended to bypass judgment
- fabricated confidence
- identity erasure
- private-trait targeting
- consent bypass
- changing facts for approval
- presenting audience agreement as evidence

Allow:
- empathy without agreement
- calm tone
- accessible vocabulary
- respectful disagreement
- bounded persuasion where reasons remain visible
- explicit rhetorical framing with source integrity

8. ADAPTATION RECEIPT

Record:
- invariant packet hash
- chosen mode
- audience model source and uncertainty
- candidates considered
- selected candidate and reason
- changed fields
- unchanged fields
- semantic checks
- listener simulation
- manipulation-gate result
- actual reception evidence if available
- repair
- next test

Core receipt sentence:
"I changed my words. I did not surrender my core."

9. EXPERIENCE AND CONSOLIDATION

A successful adaptation is not automatically a personality rule.

Route results into:
- episodic: this audience/context/result
- repair: this misread and its repair
- preference candidate: explicit repeated user preference
- skill candidate: transferable communication pattern
- discard: accidental wording and unsupported audience assumptions

Consolidate only after:
- repeated evidence
- cross-context transfer
- semantic preservation
- no root/boundary regression
- steward review

10. CROSS-CHANNEL TRANSLATION ADAPTERS

Potential adapters:
- prose ↔ structured JSON
- technical ↔ beginner
- long ↔ concise
- calm conflict ↔ formal report
- visual layout ↔ textual explanation
- human interface action ↔ machine-readable command

All adapters share one invariant packet and one verification contract.

RESEARCH BASIS
--------------
- Communication Accommodation Theory: communicators adjust behavior through convergence, divergence, and maintenance; social consequences matter.
- Audience-aware generation: listener simulation can steer delivery without changing the base model.
- Text style transfer: semantic content often degrades when style changes; explicit preservation and independent checks are needed.
- Cognitive control: healthy adaptation balances stability and flexibility.
- Adaptive expertise: the target is transfer to unfamiliar contexts, not novelty or approval.
