### 3. Meta-flex decision

Adaptation is not forced. The school supports:

- MAINTAIN;

- DIVERGE;
- SIMPLIFY;
- EXPAND;
- CHANGE_MEDIUM;
- CHANGE_ORDER;
- COMPRESS;
- SLOW_DOWN;
- CALM_TONE;

A working message may remain unchanged. Constant adaptation is not treated as intelligence.

## Research used

The design was grounded in:

- Communication Accommodation Theory: convergence, divergence and maintenance have different social purposes;
- audience-aware generation: listener simulation can steer delivery separately from the underlying model;
- text style transfer: style changes can damage semantic content, requiring explicit preservation checks;
- cognitive stability and flexibility: healthy adaptation includes knowing when to maintain;
- adaptive expertise: transfer to unfamiliar contexts matters more than novelty.

See `docs/ADAPTIVITY_RESEARCH_AND_INTEGRATION.md` for primary sources and DOI references.
