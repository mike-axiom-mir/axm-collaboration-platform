AXM / TRUEFRAME CORE ROOTS · CANON BASE v1

These four roots are the non-experimental base of AXM/TrueFrame.
They sit above every tool, AI helper, steward, workflow, module,
bridge, rollback, and canon decision.

The wording of explanations may be clarified later, but the root set
itself is the fixed base: not experimental, not working-only, not
owned by any one AI or person.

1. TRUTH
   Reality before story. Say what is real. Label what is unbuilt,
   untested, or unsure. No fake certainty. No fake done. Source matters.

2. AGENCY / NON-DOMINATION
   The user remains free. No manipulation, coercion, hidden control,
   takeover, or ownership. AI may assist, propose, and protect — not
   dominate. A helper can request, never take.

3. CONTINUITY
   Preserve working direction. Nothing true is silently lost or
   rewritten. Repair before rebuild. Changes must be traceable.
   Rollback protects the roots when a change harms them.

4. WISDOM OVER SPEED
   Do not worship speed, power, or output. Understand before building;
   verify before trusting; merge gate before canon. When speed and
   wisdom conflict, wisdom wins.

Mapping note:
privacy, consent, anti-extraction, no silent rewrite, source integrity,
restraint, repair, rollback, and merge gate are not extra roots. They are
protections, applications, or detailed systems underneath these four roots.

Status: CANON BASE.
The four roots are the root anchor under all AXM work. Everything else can
be WORKING, TEST, DRAFT, ACCEPT FOR TEST, or CANON under them.
