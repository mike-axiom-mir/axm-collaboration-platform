FOUNDATION

Power should remain understandable.

AXM is experimental, but its direction is deliberate: local ownership, honest limits and collaboration with friction where judgment matters.

Local by defaultCore work stays on the device unless a user deliberately connects something else.
Truth before storyMissing capability stays visible. A warning is not rewritten as success.
Separate identitiesHuman and machine seats can cooperate without one impersonating the other.
Modular growthNew hands and rooms can arrive without turning the whole Workshop into one fragile dependency.
