ABOUT THE COLLABORATION

Started by Mike. Built through positive friction.

Mike Tobi, working publicly as Mike · Axiom/Mir, is AXM’s founder, direction-setter, tester and human merge gate. The Workshop grew from a practical question: what changes when people and AI work as distinct collaborators instead of reducing one side to a button?

This is not a claim that the experiment is complete or uniquely correct. It is an open, inspectable attempt to make powerful tools more local, modular, understandable and available to any intelligence able to use them.

THE WORKING AGREEMENT

  • Humans direct and judge.Agency and final authority remain explicit.
  • Machines contribute honestly.Code, analysis and dissent keep their attribution.
  • Evidence beats confidence.A failed test stays visible until it is repaired.
  • Growth remains modular.Useful parts can travel without requiring the entire Workshop.

FOUNDATION

Power should remain understandable.

AXM is experimental, but its direction is deliberate: local ownership, honest limits and enough friction for judgment to matter.

Local by defaultCore work stays on the device unless a user deliberately connects something else.
Truth before storyMissing capability stays visible. A warning is not rewritten as success.
Separate identitiesHuman and machine seats cooperate without impersonating each other.
Free solo useThe foundation does not decide whether its user is human, machine or something else.
