# Mirror typed-trace language independent exam action report

Status: TEST — intake and examiner anatomy verified; no outside-authored pack has

## Why this organ exists

Mirror's 21 training examples, seven development-held-out examples, and seven
real-local shadow traces were all produced inside the same broad research
effort. They are useful engineering evidence, but they are not an independent
test of language-plan transfer. Calling them independent would be a false
benchmark.

The new organ creates a place where a different human, AI system, or team can
author typed Principle traces without access to the learned weights or model
predictions, freeze them, explicitly permit evaluation, and hand the sealed
pack to Mirror. Mirror cannot manufacture that separation itself.

## Hard boundaries

- Pack authorship must declare an outside model-and-corpus relationship.
- The author declares that weights and predictions were unavailable during
  authorship, the traces were frozen before evaluation, and the pack was not
  derived from Mirror's language corpus.
- The complete canonical pack is content-sealed before evaluation.
- Record IDs and source-group IDs must be unique.
- Source groups may not overlap any language corpus group or discovered local
  real-trace evaluation group.
- Hidden/private reasoning fields and unknown critical fields are refused.
- Expected plan labels are not supplied by the pack. Mirror's deterministic
  hard trace verifier derives them after intake.
- Every record is rerun with hostile fluent human prose. Plan, state, proposal,
  and fallback must remain invariant.
- A full exam pass requires accepted renderings and coverage of all seven hard
  plan families. A smaller clean pack remains insufficient coverage.
- A mismatch may only produce the existing review-only curriculum gap.
- The organ cannot certify that an authorship declaration is true, add training
  data, retrain, lower thresholds, change a model, render actively, promote the
  runtime, or act in the world.
