## Hardcoded organ boundary

The stable hardcoded part is deliberately small and reusable. It recognizes
document shapes, extracts explicit slots, and compares those slots with the
relations already frozen in a provider-declaration research exam. Requirement
IDs are inputs, not compiled rules.

The bounded reader:

- scans only `Workshop/shared/**/*.json`;
- reads at most 512 documents;
- reads at most 2 MiB per document;
- does not follow symbolic links;
- records malformed, symbolic, and oversized sources as visible refused
  evidence;
- hashes every document before assessment;
- assesses every document independently;
- never merges one document's identity with another document's implementation,
  selectors, permissions, or boundaries.

The five recognized pattern families are:

- `axm.shared-service-contract/v1`;
- `axm.shared-engine-contract/v1`;
- `axm.foundation-service-plane/v1`;
- `axm.foundation-service-integration/v1`;
- relevant JSON Schema declarations whose IDs explicitly concern providers,
  services, foundations, or contracts.

Unknown typed and untyped JSON documents remain visible but are not silently
promoted to provider-declaration evidence.
