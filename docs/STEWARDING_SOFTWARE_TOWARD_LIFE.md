# Stewarding software toward life

Preserved from the Mike + AXM/Mir collaboration on 2026-07-23.

## The instruction that changes the trajectory

Do not merely ask a machine to **build a tree**. Ask it to **steward a software tree toward life**.

"Build a tree" rewards a finished-looking asset. It can stop at a trunk, leaves and a convincing silhouette. "Steward this tree toward life" keeps exposing the next dishonest abstraction: roots without soil exchange, leaves without light, carbon without transport, growth without seasons, and organisms without damage, reproduction or succession.

There is no fake finish line. There are better organs and stronger evidence.

## The evidence ladder

Stewardship is not permission for unbounded complication. Every rung needs:

1. a persistent reality standard;
2. memory of the earlier rungs and their limits;
3. one concrete claim about what becomes more truthful;
4. evidence capable of proving or refuting that claim;
5. a typed, replaceable change with a bounded owner;
6. a visible receipt, failure or remaining gap;
7. no silent promotion to canon.

For a tree, a possible ladder is:

`silhouette -> roots and soil exchange -> water and carbon transport -> light response -> seasonal growth -> damage and repair -> reproduction -> population and succession`

Each rung should preserve what was already proven while making one previously hidden simplification visible.

## What this does not mean

- More code is not automatically more life.
- More organs are not automatically more truth.
- A simulation is not reality merely because it is detailed.
- Evidence is not authority, and a passing verifier is not permission to install or promote.
- A steward proposes and proves bounded growth; it does not silently rewrite the world.

## Why this belongs in AXM

This pattern already applies beyond trees: planets, cities, creatures, ecosystems, games, AI bodies and production pipelines can all be stewarded through interoperable organs. The direction stays human-given. Deterministic machinery carries repeatable work, category verifiers preserve exact claims, and reasoning is used where a new abstraction or missing organ must be discovered.

The durable principle is:

> Do not optimize toward the appearance of completion. Steward the system toward a more honest life, one evidenced rung at a time.
