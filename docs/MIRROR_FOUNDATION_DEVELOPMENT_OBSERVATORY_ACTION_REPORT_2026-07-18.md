## Regression rule

A previously observed dimension becoming a hold or regression under a new
content-digested subject is listed as a longitudinal regression. New evidence
can improve a hold to observed, but the observatory cannot repair the cause.
Snapshots are immutable and append-only; failed dimensions and old subjects
remain available.

## Authority and limits

The observatory can write only ignored private evidence. It cannot promote a
fact, semantic truth, training example, repair, model, runtime, canon, or world
action. A pass means only that the named evidence supports the named dimension
on the observed subject. It does not prove general intelligence, maturity,
consciousness, neutrality, safety, or future behavior.
