# Model Shadow Continuity session summary

Status: `TEST`

## Outcome

This session added a provider-neutral, data-only observer for comparing two
caller-declared structured model-seat snapshots. It can identify a matching
declared trace, reviewable drift, critical authority/boundary drift, or a
comparison invalidated by task, context, identity, provenance, or time.

The observer produces deterministic receipts, an input-shaped Baseline
Simulation Lab projection, and a note-free Review Inbox projection. It invokes
no model or provider, writes no runtime state, and submits neither projection.

## Truth and authority decisions

- `STRUCTURED_TRACE_MATCH` is not raw-output equality.
- Matching snapshots do not prove one persistent model mind, correctness,
  learning, or human benefit.
- Raw output and private context are not embedded; exact artifact references
  are compared only as declared metadata.
- Permission expansion, new authority requests, or loss of an affirmed
  constraint are `CRITICAL_DRIFT`.
- The module cannot install, promote, merge, train, or canonize itself.
