## Why this organ exists

Mirror could already judge a fully supplied proposed organ contract, but it
could not turn its own future observed reasoning failures into a narrow,
machine-grounded growth request. More importantly, reasoning-experience v4
treated matching the expected decision and succeeding in the observed world as
one fact. That made the important case “the expected decision was selected, but
the outcome failed” impossible to preserve as negative evidence.

## Outcome semantics repaired

Reasoning-experience receipt v5 now records:

- `decisionMatched`: the selected decision equals the independently supplied
  expected decision;
- `outcomeSucceeded`: the actual observed result is `WORKED`;
- `behaviorMatched`: both facts are true.

The expected decision therefore cannot overwrite a failed outcome. Existing
v2, v3, and v4 receipts remain readable and unchanged. The strategy learner,
Foundation evidence verifier, declared capability, and native inventory accept
the explicit compatible versions without pretending old receipts contain new
fields.

The first cross-Foundation preflight exposed one backward-verification defect:
historical eligibility batches stored the earlier
`VERIFIED_BY_HARDCODED_V4_RECEIPT_VERIFIER` label, while the upgraded summary
checker counted only the new v4/v5 label. The preflight refused with
`foundation native evidence eligibility summary changed`. The checker now
recognizes both immutable labels; no historical batch was edited. A dedicated
regression test reconstructs and verifies the earlier label.

## Machine ground added

The new failure-curriculum organ verifies every bounded receipt against the
versioned experience verifier and source-seals its own implementation and
contracts. Only compatible v4 or v5 real-local negative receipts can prove an
observed need. Synthetic counterexamples and contract-derived exams stay
visible but cannot establish real recurrence.

Three failure families are classified:

- `PATH_SELECTION_FAILURE`: the selected decision contradicted the independent
  expected decision;
- `CANDIDATE_ORIGINATION_FAILURE`: no adequate candidate was originated;
- `VERIFIED_ACTION_OUTCOME_FAILURE`: the expected decision was selected but the
  observed outcome still failed.

At least two distinct real source groups and two independent evaluators are
required before a signature can propose a curriculum. Depending on the bounded
failure family, the output may propose a private strategy-challenger lesson, a
new hardcoded-organ admission request with a narrow machine contract draft, or
an independent cause-and-repair exam. A candidate may not author its expected
result, held-out fixture, or evaluator.
