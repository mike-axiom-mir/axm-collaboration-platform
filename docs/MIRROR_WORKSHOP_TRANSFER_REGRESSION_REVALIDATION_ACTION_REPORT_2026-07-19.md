## Why a dedicated organ was earned

The existing full Workshop curriculum was not a safe examiner for this seam.
It opens runtime sessions, writes practice episodes, may append training
receipts, and may invoke learning cycles. Reusing it would have mixed diagnosis
with practice and learning.

The dedicated revalidation path instead:

- reads current Workshop JavaScript only for content inventory;
- never executes Workshop JavaScript or invokes a Workshop module;
- content-seals whole-Workshop JavaScript, shared JavaScript, shared JSON, and
  the exact module contract/manifest inputs before and after the exam;
- runs only deterministic Mirror Reasoning Foundation canaries;
- requires at least one held-out contract and one exact route;
- preserves exact expected decisions, unbound-manifest decoy rejection, and
  closed proposal-only authority;
- writes one ignored, content-addressed private exam after source stability is
  established;
- invalidates the match when Workshop source, the examiner, its schemas, its
  policy, the Foundation observer, or the deterministic cells change.
