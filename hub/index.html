Layers

Group modules into spaces — Open, Private, AI-Native, or any you name. Rename, add, delete, hide, and assign freely.

A door sign is not a lock. A layer passphrase only stops someone walking in by accident. It is not security: the files sit on this machine and the check runs in the browser. Anyone who wants in, gets in. Never put something behind it that must stay secret.

Hidden is not secret. A hidden layer (like AI-Native) keeps machine-native modules out of a human's sidebar so they aren't bothered by them. The sidebar still says how many are tucked away, and one click shows them.

Layers organise; they never change what a module is allowed to do — that comes only from its passport, the same in every layer.
