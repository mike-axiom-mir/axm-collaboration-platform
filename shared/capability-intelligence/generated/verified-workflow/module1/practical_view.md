# Explore Workshop capabilities — Practical View

## What it does

Turns source-bound machine capability declarations into plain-language explanations that people can search, filter, preview, and inspect at increasing levels of detail.

## Why use it

People can understand what a capability does, what it needs, what it produces, and what remains unknown before choosing or changing an interface.


## Required inputs

- Capability source record

## Expected outputs

- Plain-language capability preview
- Practical and deep detail views
- Source metadata

## Beginner-safe operations

- Search and preview a capability without changing it

## Common mistakes

- Treating a declared capability as runtime proof

## Limitations

- Human readability does not prove runtime behavior

## Safety and recovery

- Risk level: low
- Reversibility: fully_reversible
- Human confirmation required: False
- Safe preview recommended: False
