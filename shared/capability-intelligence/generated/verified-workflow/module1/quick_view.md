# Explore Workshop capabilities

Turns source-bound machine capability declarations into plain-language explanations that people can search, filter, preview, and inspect at increasing levels of detail.

**Why it matters:** People can understand what a capability does, what it needs, what it produces, and what remains unknown before choosing or changing an interface.

**Example:** Search for a Workshop capability, preview its quick explanation, then inspect practical details and source evidence.

**Risk:** low  
**Maturity:** tested
