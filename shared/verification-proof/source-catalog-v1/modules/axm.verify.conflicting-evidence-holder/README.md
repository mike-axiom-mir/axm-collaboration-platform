# Conflicting Evidence Holder

Detached AXM Verification & Proof organ for seed 012.

It stores evidence receipts and exposes conflicts when authoritative-looking verdicts disagree under the same claim, target profile, scope, and assertion key. Conflicts remain held even after review notes are attached; the holder has no resolution or approval authority.

## Truth boundaries

- Original receipts remain intact.
- PASS versus FAIL is held as conflict, not averaged.
- Different profiles or scopes do not become false conflicts.
- Review notes do not erase or overwrite conflict evidence.
- The organ cannot choose a winner or promote CANON.

Status: TEST-HOLD, detached, v0.1.0.
