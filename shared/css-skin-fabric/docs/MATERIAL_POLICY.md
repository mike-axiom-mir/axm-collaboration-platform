# Material Policy v0.2

Every material must state:

- Purpose and approved contexts.
- Performance tier: low, medium or high.
- Fallback material.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency behavior.
- Forced-colors behavior.
- Browser support category.
- Public tuning variables and allowed ranges.
- Visual fixture and rollback pointer before promotion.

## Current recipes

- Solid Professional — reliable default.
- Soft Glass — contextual transparency.
- Luminous Edge — selected or important region.
- Luxury Metal — structural workshop surface.
- Hologram — temporary information and preview.
- Paper/Play — tactile game surface.
- Energy Core — rare protected center or milestone.
- High Contrast — readability-first fallback.
- Aetherglass — signature layered workspace surface.
- Obsidian Ceramic — quiet premium long-session control surface.
- Signal Weave — diagnostics, routing and machine-status surface.

Energy Core remains rare. Aetherglass is for signature workspaces, not every card. Obsidian Ceramic should carry dense long-session structure. Signal Weave should communicate technical status rather than decorate unrelated content.

When everything glows, nothing has hierarchy.
