# AXM Game Asset Quality Ladder

The ladder prevents “more assets” from being confused with “better games.” PS2 is the minimum public presentation floor. PS3 and PS4 are deliberate later capability tiers, not labels that can be granted from polygon counts alone.

## Tier 1 - PS2 / 2002-2006 minimum (active)

A public candidate must have all of the following:

- a coherent original art-direction profile and a complete gameplay family, not isolated demos;
- readable silhouettes at gameplay distance and geometry density floors for every category;
- facades with frames, sills, entrances, roof detail and category-specific features;
- vehicles with shaped cabins, glazing, wheels/hubs, lights, bumpers, mirrors, plates and variant-specific detail;
- characters with separated limbs, face/hair/clothing cues, a 13-joint minimum skin, and animation on the same mesh shown to reviewers;
- a 1024 atlas with at least 32 semantically distinct materials plus day, dusk, night and rain variants;
- strict LODs, collision candidates, navigation candidates, runtime-sized textures, and an engine-neutral manifest;
- a composed gameplay scene, contact sheets, and an interactive preview reviewed at original resolution;
- human visual approval and native-engine evidence before public promotion.

The current generator may issue `PS2_TECHNICAL_CANDIDATE_PASS`. It may not set visual approval, runtime approval, or public release approval itself.

## Tier 2 - PS3 / 2006-2013 target (future)

Build our own dependency-free equivalents for:

- tangent-space normal and specular/gloss authoring with higher-frequency surface detail;
- 2K atlases, texture streaming, material instances, decals, alpha-tested foliage and emissive windows;
- approximately 5K-20K triangle hero characters/vehicles with improved deformation, IK, root motion and animation blending;
- baked lightmaps, dynamic shadow cascades, ambient probes, reflection probes and post-processing parity;
- occlusion culling, streaming sectors, impostors/HLODs and measured memory/performance budgets;
- richer modular interiors, destructible prop states, damage variants and crowd/traffic variation;
- native engine import, shader parity and frame-time evidence on representative scenes.

## Tier 3 - PS4 / 2013-2020 target (future)

Build our own dependency-free equivalents for:

- physically based metallic/roughness authoring, image-based lighting, HDR pipelines and calibrated tone mapping;
- high-poly-to-low-poly baking, 4K/virtual texture paths, layered materials, trim sheets and vertex blending;
- high-detail characters with facial joints or morph targets, improved skinning, cloth/hair simulation candidates and motion matching research;
- dense environment kits, procedural scattering, photogrammetry-style reconstruction workflows and authored destruction layers;
- asynchronous asset streaming, aggressive HLOD, GPU-driven visibility, instancing and platform quality profiles;
- automated cinematic/gameplay visual regression, perceptual quality scoring and representative hardware performance gates.

## Promotion rule

Advancing tiers requires evidence on five separate surfaces: artifact structure, native runtime behavior, original-resolution visuals, representative performance, and human art/gameplay approval. Passing one surface never substitutes for another.
