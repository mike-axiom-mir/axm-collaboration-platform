# AXM Review Constitution

This layer makes review optional, explicit and identity-neutral.

- A solo constitution may require no votes. Its output remains fully usable and is labeled `UNREVIEWED_BY_CHOICE`.
- A shared project may name any set of independent review seats and decide which actions they govern.
- Identity `kind` is descriptive only. Human, machine, collective, animal, alien and unknown future forms receive no different authority from this core.
- Votes bind to the exact artifact digest. A changed artifact needs fresh judgment.
- One identity cannot silently fill multiple independent seats.
- Governance controls only the actions named in `governedActions`. It never becomes a general permission, compute or creation gate.
- Review-window size is per goal and represents available attention for that direction. Many goals may each have a small queue; one noisy goal cannot consume every review slot. A full review window must not delete or prohibit creation outside that queue.

Body Pulse protects the physical substrate. Review Constitution protects the
meaning of a chosen promotion decision. They are deliberately separate.
