current Grounded Growth knowledge frontier. It verifies the knowledge frontier,
research outcome, LIVE named-local protocol, answer-free participant packet,
cycle ancestry, v2 intervention link, and readiness route before exposing a
reviewable optional handoff.

waiting to `REVIEW_OPTIONAL_RESEARCH_HUMAN_HANDOFF_OR_WAIT`. Review is not
participation. Packet readiness is not human evidence. A later human outcome
still requires a separate voluntary opt-in, complete or withdrawn session,
explicit scoped judgment, source declaration, current closure, and native
bridge package.

installation, promotion, merge, Foundation, or `CANON` authority.
