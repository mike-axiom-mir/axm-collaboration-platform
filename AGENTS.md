# AXM Mirror research rules

Mirror is research material until Mike explicitly accepts a tested result.

Roots:

1. Truth before story.
2. Agency and non-domination.
3. Continuity and source lineage.
4. Wisdom over speed.
5. No fake done.

Rules:

- Preserve machine state and human rendering as two views of one trace.
- Never let human-sounding prose become hidden decision authority.
- Never let learned weights rewrite roots, permissions, evidence, or release gates.
- Never grant a tool or write permission because a component is called AI-native.
- Treat unknown, contradictory, and untested as valid states.
- Append or supersede; do not silently erase failed evidence.
- Generated outputs are proposals until verified.
- Keep secrets, tokens, state, private memories, datasets, and checkpoints out of commits.
- Explain changed files, tests run, tests not run, and known limits.

Status labels: EXPERIMENTAL, TEST, WORKING, KNOWN_FAIL, NEEDS_REVIEW, CANON.
Only Mike may accept CANON.
