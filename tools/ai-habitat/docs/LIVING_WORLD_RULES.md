# Living World Rules

The Habitat may be entertaining and expressive without pretending that every animation, face, or line is intelligence.

## Evidence-linked elements

These must come from state, a bridge packet, or proof:

- avatar presence and connection status;
- room location;
- working, playing, waiting, blocked, uncertain, verifying, or idle state;
- state-face mapped from that declared state;
- voluntary expression submitted by the seat;
- explicit silence response;
- carried object label and active route;
- permission request;
- game board and move history;
- artifact creation and action result;
- proof event.

## Suggested but inactive material

These may be visible as examples but must remain labelled as not spoken:

- quote-library lines;
- category face samples;
- possible check-ins;
- possible game jokes;
- possible repair or uncertainty statements.

## Decorative elements

These may animate for atmosphere but must not alter or imply state:

- room furniture;
- ambient light;
- corridor pulses;
- background grid;
- idle room glow;
- particles after a verified event;
- optional interface sounds.

Turning ambience off does not disconnect seats or stop work.

## Speech and silence

- An invitation is optional.
- External seats never receive an invented reply.
- No response remains no response.
- An explicit silence packet is preserved separately.
- Clearing a stale bubble is not described as chosen silence.
- State-faces continue to show declared state even when words are quiet, unless the human disables them.
- A visible face or line does not grant authority.

## Avatar freedom and permission

A future AI seat may choose among available safe activities and may choose whether to use the signal channel, but movement and expression are not authority. Consequential actions remain gated and external side effects still require executor evidence.
