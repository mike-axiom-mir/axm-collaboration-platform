# Safety and Truth Boundaries

## Visible does not mean authorized

An avatar entering a room or showing a face is a visible state transition. It does not grant file access, publishing rights, money access, messaging authority, root modification, or operating-system control.

Sensitive actions and rooms outside the seat's approved room list go to the Permission Gate.

## Face does not mean feeling

The signal faces are symbolic machine-state shorthand. They can be funny and useful without claiming human emotion, sentience, pain, joy, preference, or inner experience.

## Suggested quote does not mean spoken quote

Lines in `config/expression_library.json` are local examples. The interface labels them “examples only.” A line becomes active only when:

- a real connected seat submits it; or
- a built-in demo seat accepts an invitation through the explicitly labelled deterministic demo choice hand.

The phrase library is not Mirror canon and does not silently train or rewrite Mirror.

## Invitation does not mean obligation

A connected seat may answer with words, a face only, explicit silence, or nothing. External seats are never auto-answered by the Habitat.

Unknown, expired, reused, and wrong-seat invitation IDs are rejected. A no-words invitation cannot be upgraded into speech by the bridge.

## Silence outcomes remain distinct

- ignored invitation: no response record;
- explicit silence: preserved as a voluntary response;
- expired expression: display timed out;
- cleared expression: human removed the current display.

The system does not silently turn one into another.

## Demo does not mean live AI

The package contains deterministic demo hands for art, code, notes, tests, relays, Connect Four, and optional signal demonstrations. They prove the architecture and are source-labelled. They do not claim a hidden live provider made the choice.

## Decorative life does not mean activity

Room props, glows, particles, routes without an action, and sounds are interface presentation. They never count as completed work and never alter permissions or proof.

## Mistakes remain distinguishable from dishonesty

The proof trail preserves failures, denials, losses, and test errors. Ordinary errors are not reclassified as deception without evidence.

## No privileged desktop control yet

Mimic Grip currently carries visible labels. Demo executors write only under `runtime/artifacts/`. They do not grab, edit, move, or delete arbitrary computer files.

## Network boundary

The server binds to `127.0.0.1`. External writers need the local bridge key; browser writes require the exact local origin. Read endpoints are local but unauthenticated in this prototype. Do not expose the server to LAN or internet without encrypted transport, device enrollment, revocation, rate limits, and a deliberate remote-access design.

## Canon boundary

The Habitat is an interface, action, and communication architecture. It does not change AXM roots or promote learned material into canon. `change_roots` remains a sensitive action and is never performed by the demo hand.
