# AXM Precanonical Signal Metabolism Policy

## Principle

Everything received may be preserved as **signal** by default. Nothing received becomes fact,
evidence, proof, priority, execution authority, or CANON merely because it was received.

This makes mistakes non-breaking without making the system gullible.

## Signal maturation

`RAW -> NORMALIZED -> LINKED -> {CORROBORATED | DISCONFIRMED | CONFLICTED | INSUFFICIENT_DATA}`

The original signal is never rewritten into evidence. A separate evidence record or test must be
created under the stable shared contract if a claim deserves evidence status.

## Wrong-but-useful rule

A disconfirmed signal stays retained. It may expose:

- a missing validator;
- a hidden assumption;
- a version conflict;
- a research question;
- a need candidate;
- a weak interface or explanation;
- a missing disconfirming test.

Those derivatives begin as candidates. The wrong signal does not magically make the derivative true.

## Noise control without erasure

Exact duplicates share one deterministic signal record but retain every occurrence in the append-only
signal event stream. Attention tiers (HOT/WARM/COLD) only control what deserves scarce review attention.
They never change truth or delete low-ranked signals.

## Merge rule

A package may fail active merge while its intake metadata is still preserved as signal. This is the
normal response to conflicts, unsupported versions, malformed references, or forbidden authority
requests: **do not merge; do not erase the observation**.

## Yield rule

Later, signal usefulness is measured from evidenced outcomes. Correctness and usefulness are separate:
a correct signal may create no value, while a mistaken signal may reveal a missing guard. No HELPED,
NO_EFFECT, or HARMED label is assigned without outcome evidence.
