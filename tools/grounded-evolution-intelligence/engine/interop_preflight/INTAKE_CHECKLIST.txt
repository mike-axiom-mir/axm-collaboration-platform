3. Produce one axm.three-module-intake-manifest/v1 per package.

6. Module IDs must be exactly:

8. Duplicate record IDs are allowed only when kind and digest are identical.
9. Any different-digest collision goes to CONFLICT_HOLD; never last-writer-wins.
10. Preflight is dry-run only. It grants no execution, promotion, merge, or CANON authority.
11. After preflight PASS, inspect warnings and merge plan before any canonical intake.
12. After canonical intake, rebuild the graph and rerun all diagnosis detectors.
