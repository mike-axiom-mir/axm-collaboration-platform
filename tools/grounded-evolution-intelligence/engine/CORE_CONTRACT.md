# AXM Grounded Evolution Intelligence — Core Contract

## Purpose

Maintain a grounded, reconstructable model of AXM and convert verified gaps into explainable
research, build, repair, integration, documentation, simplification, reuse, test, deprecation,
or wait-for-evidence proposals.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Facts, declarations, inferences, and hypotheses remain visibly different.
2. No proposal becomes canon without an explicit steward decision.
3. No artifact is called an improvement solely because it exists.
4. Improvement claims require retained baseline and outcome evidence.
5. Evidence keeps source location, hash, capture time, extraction method, limitations, and scope.
6. Unknowns and contradictions remain visible.
7. Append-only events are the source of reconstructable state.
8. Derived state may be deleted and rebuilt; event history may not be silently rewritten.
9. AI reasoning may recommend but may not promote its own inference into observation.
10. Human and authorized steward decisions remain reversible and are logged with reasons.
11. Imports preserve unsupported fields rather than deleting information they do not understand.
12. Automated imports never promote DECLARED to TESTED or REPRODUCED.
13. The active direction is preserved unless an explicit decision changes it.
14. Safety, accessibility, meaning, and long-term architecture may override numerical priority.

## Layer separation

### Deterministic factual kernel
Stores records, evidence, events, relationships, and derived state that can be reconstructed.

### Reasoning and direction layer
Produces proposals. Its outputs begin as HYPOTHESIS or INFERRED and require review and proof.

## Current authority boundary

Version 0.1.0 defines exchange and validation rules. It does not authorize autonomous execution,
self-modification, deletion, deprecation, or canonization.
