# Truth States

- **OBSERVED** — directly present in retained evidence.
- **DECLARED** — claimed by a module, human, document, or model; not independently proven.
- **TESTED** — supported by a retained test with identifiable conditions and result.
- **REPRODUCED** — independently repeated with materially matching results.
- **INFERRED** — reasoned from evidence but not directly observed.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible proposal awaiting evidence.
- **CONFLICTED** — credible records disagree.
- **OBSOLETE** — superseded but retained for reconstruction and history.
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence is insufficient.

## Promotion rules

DECLARED -> TESTED requires a linked evidence record of source type TEST or MEASUREMENT.
TESTED -> REPRODUCED requires an independently identifiable repeated result.
INFERRED and HYPOTHESIS cannot be promoted by wording changes; new evidence is required.
CONFLICTED remains until the disagreement is explicitly resolved or scoped.
