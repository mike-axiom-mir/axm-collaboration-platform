======================================================================
1. MODULE IDENTITY
======================================================================

Canonical name:
AXM Grounded Evolution Intelligence

Working nickname:
The Elder Sibling

Primary purpose:
Maintain a grounded, reconstructable model of the AXM ecosystem; determine
what should be researched, built, repaired, integrated, tested, simplified,
documented, reused, deprecated, or postponed; and verify whether completed
work created real improvement.

Core question:
What does AXM actually contain, what does it genuinely know, what does each
part still need, what direction has the greatest justified value, and what
changed because of previous actions?

This is not merely another analysis module. It is a system-of-systems
evolution layer for scalable, evidence-grounded growth.

======================================================================
2. ROOT CONTRACT
======================================================================

The module must preserve the following roots:

- Truth before story.
- No fake certainty.
- No fake progress.
- No silent rewrite.
- No automatic canon.
- Source integrity.
- Evidence must remain traceable.
- Human and steward agency remain intact.
- AI may recommend but may not disguise inference as fact.
- History must remain reconstructable.
- Unknowns must stay visible.
- Contradictions must be recorded rather than smoothed over.
- Improvement claims require before-and-after evidence.
- A new artifact is not automatically an improvement.
- Wisdom and system value matter alongside speed and quantity.
- The active architecture must be preserved unless an explicit decision
  changes it.

======================================================================
3. RELATIONSHIP TO THE OTHER TWO NEW MODULES
======================================================================

AXM Human Capability Atlas
- Explains what capabilities are.
- Converts machine declarations into human-readable, teachable, searchable,
  interface-ready Capability Cards.

AXM Human Interface Intelligence
- Determines how humans should safely and effectively interact with
  capabilities.
- Selects suitable interface patterns, beginner layers, controls, explanations,
  and interaction modes.

AXM Grounded Evolution Intelligence
- Determines what the ecosystem needs next and why.
- Tracks dependencies, weaknesses, evidence, research gaps, build directions,
  and verified outcomes.

Shared loop:

WHAT EXISTS
    -> Human Capability Atlas

HOW HUMANS INTERACT
    -> Human Interface Intelligence

WHAT EVOLVES NEXT
    -> Grounded Evolution Intelligence

RESULTS RETURN INTO THE SYSTEM
    -> Updated capabilities, interfaces, evidence, needs, tests, and direction

The three modules must remain independently usable but share compatible
identifiers, evidence states, and exchange formats.

======================================================================
4. HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

The module has two strictly separated layers.

A. DETERMINISTIC FACTUAL KERNEL

Stores only reconstructable records supported by source artifacts, test
results, manifests, logs, hashes, or explicit declarations.

Responsibilities:
- Register modules and versions.
- Register capabilities.
- Register dependencies and relationships.
- Register evidence and source locations.
- Register tests and observed results.
- Register limitations, risks, gaps, and blockers.
- Register research questions and obtained knowledge.
- Register interventions and outcomes.
- Rebuild current state from append-only event history.
- Detect contradictions, missing evidence, and stale records.

The deterministic kernel must not silently invent facts or overwrite history.

B. REASONING AND DIRECTION LAYER

Uses the factual kernel to generate proposals.

Allowed outputs:
- BUILD
- REPAIR
- RESEARCH
- TEST
- INTEGRATE
- DOCUMENT
- SIMPLIFY
- DEPRECATE
- REUSE
- WAIT FOR EVIDENCE

Every proposal must identify:
- What should happen.
- Why it is needed.
- Evidence supporting the need.
- Affected modules and capabilities.
- Expected benefit.
- Estimated cost.
- Regression or safety risk.
- Required knowledge.
- Verification method.
- Confidence and truth status.

Reasoning outputs remain proposals or hypotheses until verified.

======================================================================
6. TRUTH AND EVIDENCE STATES
======================================================================

Every important statement must carry one of these states:

- OBSERVED
  Directly found in an artifact, manifest, log, test output, or measurement.

- DECLARED
  Claimed by a module, builder, document, or AI but not independently proven.

- TESTED
  Supported by a known test with retained test details.

- REPRODUCED
  Independently repeated with matching results.

- INFERRED
  Reasoned from evidence but not directly observed.

- HYPOTHESIS
  Plausible proposal awaiting evidence.

- CONFLICTED
  Reliable sources or observations disagree.

- OBSOLETE
  Superseded but preserved for history and reconstruction.

- UNKNOWN
  Insufficient evidence.

Evidence records should include:
- evidence_id
- source_type
- source_location
- source_hash
- extraction_method
- timestamp
- relevant_scope
- confidence
- limitations
- linked_claims

======================================================================
7. DETERMINISTIC EVOLUTION CYCLE
======================================================================

1. OBSERVE
   Read manifests, capability declarations, code metadata, tests, logs,
   intervention records, research results, and source hashes.

2. NORMALIZE
   Convert observations into shared schemas, identifiers, units, and
   relationship types.

3. VALIDATE
   Check whether claims are supported, current, reproducible, and internally
   consistent.

4. MAP
   Rebuild module, capability, dependency, interface, evidence, and need
   graphs.

5. DIAGNOSE
   Detect weak links, blocked goals, missing proof, duplicated work,
   integration opportunities, stale knowledge, and unresolved contradictions.

6. PRIORITIZE
   Rank possible actions using visible factors rather than hidden judgment.

7. PROPOSE
   Generate research, build, repair, test, integration, documentation,
   simplification, reuse, deprecation, or wait directions.

8. SELECT
   Human or authorized steward accepts, rejects, modifies, defers, or combines
   directions.

9. EXECUTE
   External builders, agents, tools, or humans perform the selected work.

10. VERIFY
    Compare the expected result against observed evidence and retained tests.

11. LEARN
    Update confidence, evidence, needs, patterns, and direction quality.

12. ARCHIVE
    Append the full trace without rewriting history.

13. REBUILD
    Confirm that the current system state can be reconstructed from records.

    * expected_usefulness
    * unblock_value
    * reuse_potential

priority_denominator =
    estimated_cost
    * regression_risk
    * uncertainty_penalty

The formula is advisory, not sovereign.

Requirements:
- Raw factor values remain visible.
- Weighting is versioned.
- Steward overrides are recorded with reasons.
- Meaning, dignity, safety, accessibility, and long-term architectural value
  may override a purely numerical rank.
- The system must never collapse all quality dimensions into one hidden score.

11. VERIFICATION AND ANTI-FAKE-DONE GATES
======================================================================

Gate A — Reconstruction
Can current state be rebuilt from retained events and sources?

Gate B — Traceability
Can every important claim be traced to evidence?

Gate C — Separation
Are facts, declarations, inferences, and hypotheses visibly distinct?

Gate D — Repeatability
Does the same input state produce the same deterministic diagnosis?

Gate E — Outcome proof
Does an intervention record show before and after evidence?

Gate F — Regression awareness
Did the system check for harmed modules or new limitations?

Gate G — Unknown preservation
Are unresolved unknowns still visible?

Gate H — Human agency
Can a steward reject, modify, or override a recommendation with a logged
reason?

Gate I — No silent canon
Did any proposal become architecture without explicit acceptance?

Gate J — Real usefulness
Did the completed action unlock, improve, simplify, protect, or clarify
something measurable or demonstrable?

A phase is not complete merely because files exist.

12. FAILURE MODES TO DESIGN AGAINST
======================================================================

- Large archive with no usable reasoning.
- AI-generated relationships treated as facts.
- Duplicate module identities.
- Version confusion.
- Hidden score determining direction.
- Quantity rewarded over value.
- Cosmetic changes labeled as system improvement.
- Tests that only confirm their own implementation.
- Evidence links lost during import.
- Old research presented as current truth.
- Conflicts silently resolved.
- Recommendations with no verification plan.
- Research conducted without a decision it can unlock.
- Automatic deprecation of meaningful experimental work.
- Central module becoming a domination point.
- Excessive complexity making the system unusable by Mike or beginners.
- Dependency graph becoming stale.
- Autonomous self-improvement claims without external proof.
- Event history being rewritten to make results look cleaner.

15. INITIAL SUCCESS DEFINITION
======================================================================

The module becomes genuinely useful when it can inspect a limited but real
sample of AXM and produce a result similar to:

"Module X declares capability Y, but the available evidence only supports part
of that claim. Modules A, B, and C depend on Y. This makes verification of Y a
higher-value action than building another isolated feature. Required next
action: create test Z. Expected cost: low. Regression risk: low. Decision
unlocked: whether Y can become a shared foundation. After execution, tests
show that two dependent modules improved, one remained unchanged, and one new
limitation appeared."

That is grounded scalable evolution.

======================================================================
16. LONG-TERM ROLE
======================================================================

The AXM Grounded Evolution Intelligence module should eventually become:

- the factual map of the collaboration platform;
- the memory of why architectural decisions were made;
- the detector of weak or missing evidence;
- the generator of research and build directions;
- the tracker of real versus claimed improvement;
- the bridge between modules, capabilities, interfaces, tests, and knowledge;
- the steward assistant for deciding where limited human and AI attention
  should go next.

It must not become:
- the owner of AXM;
- an unquestionable authority;
- a hidden optimizer;
- an autonomous canon engine;
- a replacement for human meaning or steward judgment.

Its deepest role is to help AXM grow without losing truth, context, evidence,
or direction as the ecosystem becomes too large for any single human or AI
session to hold reliably.
