# Relaybound asset sources

All art assets listed below are used under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

- Quaternius **RPG Character Pack**: Warrior and Wizard models, rigs, textures, and animation clips. https://quaternius.com/packs/rpgcharacters.html
- Quaternius **Cute Animated Monsters Pack**: Demon model and animation clips. https://quaternius.com/packs/cutemonsters.html
- Kenney **Modular Dungeon Kit**: environment GLB models. https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-dungeon-kit

Quaternius states that all models are CC0 and may be modified or combined without attribution. Kenney labels the Modular Dungeon Kit as CC0. Copies of source license text are kept beside the relevant packaged assets where supplied.

Three.js 0.160.0 runtime modules are vendored locally under their MIT license.
