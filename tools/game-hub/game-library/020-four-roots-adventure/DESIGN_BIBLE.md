# Design Bible

The player carries four seals through an authored order: Truth, Agency /
non-domination, Continuity, then Wisdom over speed. Each seal is earned through
a different interaction grammar rather than a reskinned collectible:

- Truth keeps two contradictory witnesses separate and yields bounded
  uncertainty.
- Agency releases three lanterns while preserving refusal and return.
- Continuity joins before-state, rupture, and recovery into a visible bridge.
- Wisdom asks for observation, dissent, and consequence without a timer.

The ending deliberately opens into a review room. It celebrates completion
without converting game completion into installation, promotion, or CANON.

Visual language uses luminous field markers over a dark Workshop map. Every
map actor is text-labelled in the public state and every keyboard action has a
visible button equivalent. Calm motion is the default.
