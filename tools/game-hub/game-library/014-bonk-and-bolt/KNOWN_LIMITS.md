# Known limits

This is deliberately called a **First Edition**, not an alpha or beta. It is a
complete, winnable local game shape, but it is not falsely described as a
commercial-scale finished RPG.

The current `1.1.1-universal-gamepad-test` package contains the unsealed
`1.1.0-relay-test` expansion plus the universal controller readiness seam.
Three route rehearsals and one sixth adventure are implemented and locally
verified; their long-course pacing, physical target-controller feel and replay
value still require Mike's human taste review before any WORKING or release
claim.

- The open world is a dense 320 × 320 handcrafted/procedural valley, not dozens
  of square kilometers or hundreds of authored story hours.
- The Human hero now has one licensed CC0 skinned production model with authored
  idle, locomotion and action clips. It is a provenance-safe integration rung,
  not final cast breadth: the three classes currently share that Human body and
  retain their distinct code-native weapons, cues and semantic timing. The Toon,
  NPCs, robots, enemies and environments remain original procedural 3D meshes.
- Blender is not available in the current tool lane, so this pass did not author
  facial animation, bespoke grip corrections, costume variants, or a final
  Bonk & Bolt-specific body sculpt. The primitive Human is retained as the
  deterministic runtime fallback rather than making asset loading a play gate.
- Separated browser observations prove readable action and settled states. Exact
  phase cadence is deterministic test evidence because no rolling-frame capture
  capability was available.
- Co-op is same-device shared-camera co-op. Its no-teleport camera covenant
  begins soft outward damping at 26m and holds the shared view at 36m with a
  visible tether and explicit regroup state; it is not split-screen. There is no
  LAN or internet networking, matchmaking, rollback, or separate-device
  synchronization.
- Browser localStorage is the world-save authority. The local server owns launch
  and health metadata, not authoritative combat simulation.
- The 23-hour omen, 24-hour finale, persistent ruin and bonded-pet resistance
  transitions are deterministic, systems-tested and staged in live browser
  verification. A literal uninterrupted 24-hour browser soak has not
  been run.
- Keyboard and mouse behavior is part of the live browser check. Physical
  gamepad feel still needs a person holding the target controller.
- The Meadow Curfew Circuit was completed through visible keyboard play, and a
  Two-Town Nightline checkpoint survived two reloads. The full Two-Town and Vale
  Companion courses have deterministic contracts and rendered gates but have
  not yet received end-to-end human completion/pacing passes.
- Sound is synthesized from Web Audio. There is no authored soundtrack, voice
  performance, foley library, or final mix.
- Final balance, humor, pacing, camera comfort, long-session save reliability,
  and whether fights are genuinely memorable require Mike and another human
  player—not a self-awarded automated status.
- The generated title image is a local project asset. The external tool used to
  create it is not called at runtime and does not add moderation, prompt limits,
  accounts, or filters to the workshop or game.
