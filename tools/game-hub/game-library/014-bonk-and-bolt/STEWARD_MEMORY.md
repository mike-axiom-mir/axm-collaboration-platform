## The promise of this game

- Bonk & Bolt is its own separate game slot. Do not mix it with the other builders' games.
- It is a polished 3D cartoon open-world action RPG with an elevated, readable Brawl Stars-like camera—not a painted or flat-looking game.
- It supports solo and two-player local co-op. Network co-op is not currently promised.
- There is no conventional main quest. Every clear side adventure should feel small at first and then reveal part of the larger story.
- Fights must be mechanically readable and funny enough that winning or losing creates a story worth remembering.
- Equipment should create unusual choices, tradeoffs, manipulation, and build identity. Avoid junk floods; auto-salvage is important.
- Avoid consumable pressure and short “next few hits” boosts. Meal effects can last around an hour and should change play rather than merely add percentage chores.
- Cooking and fishing need long-term meaning, authored challenges, rivals, and world consequences—not 1,000 repetitions for a tiny level increase.
- Challenges must state what to do and where to do it. No hidden puzzle, lucky-discovery, or internet-guide requirements.
- Miniature robots begin neutral and have world jobs, villages, rivalries, command/hijack interactions, and late-game pet depth.
- Rare resonance stones appear through long-term map play. Holding one near a robot reveals its need; capture consumes one stone. Holding two consumes both; holding three or more still consumes only two. This intentionally rewards either using stones or hoarding many.
- Personal robot pets can be downed and revived. Golden Ducks caught while fishing support revival and can also give health, mana, money, or rare gifts.
- After 24 saved play-hours, an enemy enters gradually. Humans and toons cannot win alone; the player must restore robot identities, select specialist brains, and help a robot team defeat the invader. Winning truly wins the game but play continues. Losing leaves a persistently damaged world with an extremely difficult recovery campaign.
