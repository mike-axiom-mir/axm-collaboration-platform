# Milestone 08 - Hushglass Commons

Status: **VERIFIED local pre-alpha slice**.

This milestone adds Hushglass House as a second persistent neighborhood household. Its need for `shared night heat` connects Crooked Kettle, the Long Table, Oola Ninehands, an owned object, and an assigned pet inside one authored Life situation. Every route, gate, immediate effect, scheduled return, and schedule override is deterministic and inspectable. Nothing in the household layer changes portal or casino odds.

## Persistent household commons

- Hushglass stores warmth, trust, agreements, history, and its last exact receipt across saves.
- Four public bands select authored state: `COLD`, `RATIONING`, `SHARED`, and `OPEN`.
- The band changes visible Hushglass structure, heat lines, and resident schedules. Hushglass overrides take precedence over the Crooked Kettle layer only where explicitly authored.
- The household is a separate resource system from family home score, supplier standing, employer standing, and storefront rating; its receipts show each crossing instead of collapsing them into a hidden score.

## Multi-party hearing routes

The `hushglass-heat-hearing` Life Thread offers the usual object, personal, and delay choices plus four disclosed routes:

- Crooked Kettle route: unlocked by supplier standing 2.
- Long Table route: unlocked by work standing 2 while rivalry pressure is at most 4.
- Oola route: unlocked by rapport 3.
- Assigned-pet audit: unlocked only when the assigned pet supplies at least 3 literal species/trait points.

The route ledger remains visible even when a route is locked. The pet route lists the exact legal species and named traits that contributed; unrelated traits remain zero.

## Exact household return

- The immediate choice applies authored warmth, trust, supplier, work, pressure, resident, pet, energy, and agreement effects and emits a non-random Life receipt.
- The scheduled `small-odds.household-accord-return/v1` resolves once on or after its due day.
- Its receipt records exact before/after values for Hushglass, Crooked Kettle, Long Table, storefront rating, parents' home, Oola, and the visible `heat-share-mobile` home mark.
- Ignoring the hearing remains a real authored outcome with its own household receipt; it is not a paused objective.

## Save and randomness contract

- Save version 8 migrates v1-v7 lives with neutral Hushglass warmth, trust, agreements, history, and route statistics. No household past is fabricated.
- Hushglass schedules are authored calendar/state rules. Household choices and returns use `random:false`, create no seed, do not consume portal charges, and do not read wealth or earlier random results.
- Portal and casino receipts retain their existing fixed-odds and replay contracts.
