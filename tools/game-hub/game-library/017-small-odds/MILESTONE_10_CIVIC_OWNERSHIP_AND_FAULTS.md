# Milestone 10 - Civic Ownership and Deterministic Faults

Status: **VERIFIED local pre-alpha slice**.

This milestone turns recurring Hushglass maintenance into a civic institution with ownership, duties, and history-derived failure. Beginning with the second due cycle, one authored fault is selected from the actual most recent maintenance return and current rivalry pressure. Service pauses until the lane chooses who holds the public obligation. Three explicit ownership models publish rights, obligations, prerequisites, immediate state changes, additive future-service effects, and a later review cycle. No fault, charter, or service effect reads or changes portal or casino entropy.

## Save-v10 civic memory

- Maintenance now persists the current governance model, selection day/cycle, next review cycle, exact charter history, active fault, and resolved fault history.
- Four deterministic fault variants are authored: retained-relay backfeed, creature-signature drift, Long Table bid-hammer, and a public date-fog fallback.
- Selection precedence is public: retained relay, then returning named pet, then Long Table route or rivalry pressure 2+, then date-fog. The frozen activation receipt names the route, asset/pet evidence, prior receipt schema, history day, and pressure.
- v1-v9 saves gain neutral civic fields without fabricated choices or fault history.

## Three ownership models

- **Communal charter:** no private owner; every household may inspect the meter and every named route may service it. Dates and returns must remain public. The model strengthens shared warmth/trust and reduces pressure on later service.
- **Hushglass household trust:** Hushglass holds a narrow night-heat veto but cannot sell the valve. Reserved hours must be disclosed and surplus heat returned. It requires trust 5 and strengthens trust/home on later service.
- **Transparent service cooperative:** Crooked Kettle and the Long Table hold a revocable service license while the lane retains the valve. Public rates and named household, creature, and tool labor are mandatory. It requires store rating 10, Kettle standing 6, and Long Table standing 3, then strengthens the production institutions while adding disclosed rivalry pressure.

Each choice uses `small-odds.commons-governance-choice/v1`, records exact immediate before/requested/actual/after values, costs posted energy and time, leaves portal odds unchanged, creates a visible home charter mark, and schedules review after two additional completed cycles. Ordinary routes remain available during an optional review window, so a review never traps the service cycle.

## Exact additive service grammar

Direct public, retained-relay, and returning-pet routes now freeze three inspectable layers: the authored route, active governance modifier, and active fault/model modifier. The summed promised return is stored at start and used exactly once at return. The declared Hushglass/Long Table order freezes and resolves the same civic layers inside its existing wage/tool/pet contract. Resolving service moves the active fault into persistent resolved history and cannot repeat it.

Locked routes remain visible. Before a required charter, all routes explain that ownership must be named and the paid Long Table override is held. After a charter, the cards show exact total consequences and a separate additive-civic-terms receipt. Retained relay ownership still never transfers; the returning pet still needs the real prior id and current literal tags.
