# SMALL ODDS · living design bible

## Promise

The player is Pip: the smallest alien in an oversized world, recently fired, newly homeless, and back in their childhood room. A machine-shell animal leads Pip to a Random Item Portal Device. The machine does not dispense guaranteed progress. It creates strange possibilities whose worth emerges through later contact with people, places, businesses, pets, accidents, and planetary events.

The lasting player story is not “I rolled a legendary.” It is “the insulting lamp I nearly sold saved my mother's dinner organism during reverse gravity, which turned our spare room into the neighborhood's first ceiling restaurant.”

## Product pillars

1. **Honest odds.** Fixed, inspectable probability; no adaptive luck, hidden pity, or outcome steering.
2. **Objects become stories.** Items carry verbs, triggers, drawbacks, social meaning, and delayed reactions.
3. **A world alive at rest.** Every scene has autonomous motion, schedules, background lives, weather, and visible consequences.
4. **Family is the home screen.** The parents' house is a physical record of choices, care, ambition, and mistakes.
5. **No quest-shaped chores.** Opportunities arise from lived situations. Players respond through activities and possessions.
6. **Comedy from systems.** The funniest outcomes are causally produced, remembered, and mechanically relevant.
7. **Style families are honest.** Cosmetic lineage is named separately and never inflated into fake mechanical uniqueness.

## Art language

Original visual thesis: **tactile domestic alien comedy × 1960s electroluminescent appliances × bioluminescent non-water coast**.

- Pip occupies roughly one third the height of an ordinary adult alien.
- Warm enamel, quilted fungus, glassy gel, oxidized metal, and soft luminous organs dominate materials.
- Rarity is behavioral: impossible shadows, nervous UI, orbiting matter, social attention, or rule-breaking motion. Border colors are secondary.
- The Glimmer is not water. It stretches, folds, remembers footsteps, and occasionally climbs architecture.
- UI is Pip's repaired pocket terminal: rounded enamel plates, amber tubes, hand-labeled switches, and imperfect scanlines.

## Item grammar

```text
physical form × core behavior × future trigger × quirk/drawback
× material × provenance × condition × mechanical rarity × cosmetic style family
```

Every generated part contributes data used by at least one action, quote, reaction, or display rule. The current catalog exceeds one billion named combinations before generated seeds, durability, value jitter, pet traits, world context, and ownership history.

## Long arc

- Spare room → repaired family home → impossible domestic laboratory → neighborhood landmark → interdimensional family estate.
- One slow portal → faster storage → parallel apertures → researched pools → ethically questionable probability infrastructure.
- Flea-market seller → specialist business → neighborhood employer → planetary market force.
- Stray shore pet → stack-trait companion ecology → inherited behaviors and unusual symbioses.
- Rumor of the Starspite casino → authentic million-to-one ticket → the now-playable first transparent-games deck → larger ship districts, social auctions, alternate routes, ownership, and probability crime at planetary scale.

## Living Threads - realized first layer

People now develop situations rather than handing Pip missions. A thread is authored, emerges deterministically from lived state, occupies a real place, offers approaches shaped by actual resources and objects, and continues if Pip stays away. Choices may return days later through relationships, money, home, reputation, item histories, and contextual reactions. The UI never uses acceptance, completion, XP, or a checklist.

The current six families are a grammar proof. Expansion should add actor schedules, dependencies between threads, business/family/pet participation, cross-location aftermath, repeat variants, and long-memory callbacks before simply multiplying map markers.

## Alien-Web Storefront - realized first economy layer

The bedroom business is now an object economy rather than a passive multiplier. A generated object can remain usable inventory, become reversible store equipment, or enter an escrow listing. An authored buyer is routed by a visible deterministic score: matching and avoided tags, one existing relationship, and a rotation tie-break. The offer then multiplies declared condition, store fit, buyer affinity, world event, reputation, relationship, equipment, branch, upgrade, and assigned-pet factors. Cash on hand, failed offers, prior random results, and whether Pip seems lucky never participate.

The player may accept, counter toward a disclosed ceiling, wait for a new day and buyer, or delist the unchanged object. A sold object leaves ownership but can return later as a buyer callback whose rating/reputation effect remembers rarity, object reaction history, packing upgrades, and the on-shift pet. This is the intended economic pattern: sellable things still become stories.

Three business identities each have two permanent branches; four common infrastructure upgrades alter listing capacity, buyer trust, callback handling, and fulfilment speed without touching portal or casino probability. Pet traits are applied literally and additively. A pet with no relevant store trait supplies company but no invented numerical bonus.

## Lopsided Lane - realized first district layer

Lopsided Lane turns item history into street life. Tavi Spool, Oola Ninehands, the Nibbin Choir, and Granduncle Latch each occupy one authored place and activity in foreglow, highglow, afterglow, and night. The schedule is a public calendar lookup, not a hidden social roll.

Residents observe explicit ownership states. A carried object is still Pip's; an escrow listing remains reversible; a gift transfers ownership and becomes relationship/home memory; a completed sale can travel through a disclosed buyer-to-resident map and become delivered neighborhood history. Reactions prefer the strongest lasting state: gifted, then sold-and-delivered, then listed, carried, and ordinary.

The district also honors the full-signature context rule. A world-event reaction appears only when the current event and the object's actual tags both match. Gift and delivery receipts name the exact item, resident, costs/gains, fixed route where relevant, ignored money boundary, and unchanged portal odds. The parents' house can acquire a thank-you pennant or parcel-periscope, but these are consequences and memories, never quest markers.

## Neighborhood Dependency Web - realized first dependency layer

The Crooked Kettle Cooperative gives Lopsided Lane one shared dependency that crosses days and systems. Its persistent standing falls into four public authored bands. A band selects resident schedule overrides and one disclosed storefront service-income multiplier; it never rolls a schedule, reads Pip's wealth, or borrows portal/casino history.

Each resident owns one repeatable arc with a relationship prerequisite, two authored responses, a known return day, and a public cooldown. Returns change resident, home, business, and supplier state exactly once and can leave a kettle-crate memory at the parents' house. The same relationships also open named neighbor approaches inside four existing Living Threads. This is the intended expansion grammar: deepen connections between existing people and places before multiplying shallow residents.

The dependency household remains deliberately bounded. Future growth should add multi-household tensions only when their arithmetic, authorship, persistence, and visual consequences can remain equally inspectable.

## Paid Neighborhood Production - realized first labor layer

The Long Table Works is both neighbor and competitor, but never a hidden difficulty director. Its three work orders follow a public day-modulo calendar. Each one asks for literal tags from an owned carried object and an assigned pet; a matching species tag and matching named traits contribute fixed labor points, while unrelated traits do nothing. The player can see the complete wage before accepting.

The cooperative approach takes longer, strengthens standing and the supplier web, and lowers pressure. The rush approach pays a public premium but raises rivalry pressure. Pressure persists through authored bands with declared wage factors. Neither approach, standing, pressure, wages, pet labor, nor work history can change portal or casino probability.

A return crosses the existing systems instead of creating a detached minigame: it pays Pip, recognizes the pet, changes one Lane relationship, touches Crooked Kettle and the storefront, and leaves a workbench stamp at home. Work standing can also open one employment-framed route inside Grift's existing memory-price Life Thread. This is the intended labor grammar: tools, creatures, relationships, and households participate through exact causal state, not abstract worker-score inflation.

## Hushglass Commons - realized second-household layer

Hushglass House keeps warmth, trust, agreements, history, and one last exact receipt as persistent state. Its `COLD`, `RATIONING`, `SHARED`, and `OPEN` bands are public authored thresholds. They change the visible building and can override resident schedules through a disclosed precedence layer; they never roll a destination or infer what would keep the player engaged.

The Hushglass heat hearing is the first multi-party Life situation. Crooked Kettle standing, Long Table standing and pressure, Oola rapport, an actually owned object, and an actually assigned pet expose different routes. Locked routes stay visible. Pet evidence uses the same literal discipline as paid production: one named species match and named traits contribute fixed points, while unrelated traits contribute zero.

The chosen route changes household and participant state immediately, then schedules one exact-once return that crosses Hushglass, supplier, work, storefront, home, resident, and visible house-mark state. Ignoring the hearing also advances the household through an authored receipt. This is the intended commons grammar: several established systems negotiate one local need through disclosed gates and exact consequences, without becoming a checklist or borrowing random language.

## Recurring Commons Service - realized living-infrastructure layer

The first accord now creates maintenance rather than a one-time victory screen. Hushglass keeps public night-valve integrity, the next service date, active work, completed and overdue counts, real participant evidence, and inspectable start/return history. `OFFLINE`, `PATCHED`, `STEADY`, and `RESILIENT` are authored bands. Due, active, and overdue states can override resident schedules through a declared precedence layer and still yield to no hidden director.

Repeat routes must reuse lived state. The public rota needs the saved returned accord; the relay route needs an actually installed or storefront-equipped matching object and never transfers ownership; the pet route needs the currently assigned creature id to appear in the original Hushglass receipt and counts its current literal tags again. The game may remember identity, ownership, work, and signatures, but it may not invent them to keep a route convenient.

The Long Table conflict is authored calendar pressure, not simulated luck. A due service date temporarily replaces the base three-order work calendar with one public valve-jig order that uses the same disclosed tool wear, literal pet labor, wage, standing, and rivalry math. Whichever service path resolves writes exact household/institution/home/resident consequences and schedules the next date. Missing the grace window creates one repairable authored consequence, never a repeat penalty loop.

## Neighborhood Debt Aftermath - realized loss-to-life layer

A casino loss is final at the table but may become a social fact outside it. Eligibility requires an unhandled saved losing receipt that replays to the same published outcome from its seed and still passes stake, winning-set, insurance, and net arithmetic checks. Cash balance, emotional framing, later luck, malformed imports, and authored artifacts cannot substitute.

The three first responses deliberately use institutions the player already knows. The Lane can publish a solidarity rota, the Long Table and Crooked Kettle can file a transparent recovery job, and Hushglass can record breathing room. All gates remain visible. Time, energy, due day, payment cap, return effect, and visual mark freeze before time moves. Each return restores at most the source loss and accounts that payment separately from storefront income; it does not erase or mutate Starspite's net ledger.

This is the intended long-tail grammar for risky play: consequences create authored work, household accommodation, social memory, and changed places instead of adaptive odds, invisible punishment, or a grindy debt meter. Additional routes should deepen named institutions and object histories before adding generic debt currencies.

## Hybrid low-poly world - realized 3D baseline

Every established location now owns an original WebGL low-poly spatial layer beneath the Canvas interaction art. The layer renders at 480x270, scales with deliberate pixelation, uses depth-tested authored cube/pyramid forms, and quantizes lighting to 16 steps. Pip, buildings, rooms, tables, stalls, the Glimmer, and durable aftermath markers have actual 3D transforms and depth.

Canvas remains the simulation and interaction authority. WebGL may enrich place, camera, silhouette, and memory but cannot hide a control or become required to continue a save. If WebGL fails, the authoritative Canvas renderer takes full opacity. Future production art should replace forms gradually while preserving this authority/fallback boundary and the 16-bit-minimum readability target.

## Stewardship rules

- Preserve save compatibility through versioned migration.
- Add mechanics through data and typed verbs before adding one-off scripted exceptions.
- Future triggers are complete tag signatures: every declared tag must be present in the context before a reaction may fire.
- Every probability change must update the visible odds, deterministic tests, and receipt schema.
- Deterministic commerce must declare every price factor, buyer-routing score, counter ceiling, and ignored personal-history input. It must never borrow probability language merely to feel dynamic.
- Authored resident schedules must remain reproducible calendar state. If schedules ever gain randomness, the exact distribution and receipt boundary must be designed and published before implementation.
- District observations must preserve ownership distinctions: escrow is not a gift, a sale is not delivered until its due-day consequence resolves, and a migrated save receives no fabricated neighborhood past.
- Resident arcs must remain authored calendar state with public prerequisites, return days, and cooldowns. Supplier factors and schedule bands must be disclosed wherever they apply; neither may read or alter probability history.
- Relationship-routed Living Thread choices must name their resident, threshold, exact effects, and delayed consequence. They are lived options, never accepted quests.
- Work orders must remain an authored public calendar. Tool ownership, literal pet matches, wage inputs, pressure factors, due day, durability cost, and exact return effects must be visible before commitment and preserved in non-random receipts.
- Pet labor must come from named species/trait tags with fixed points. Unrelated traits must contribute zero; no hidden cuteness, rarity, or global pet score may substitute.
- Household bands and schedule precedence must remain public authored state. A household route must name every supplier, employer, resident, object, pet, home, and business gate or consequence it uses.
- Household returns must resolve exactly once, preserve their immediate-choice evidence, and expose before/after values for every crossed system. A neutral migration must not fabricate agreements or household history.
- Recurring service calendars must expose due, active, grace, and overdue state. Retained-object routes must preserve ownership; returning-pet routes must cite a real earlier receipt; overdue consequences must apply once per due date and remain repairable.
- A work-calendar override must name the state that activates it and restore the base calendar afterward. Its normal tool, pet, wage, pressure, receipt, and RNG boundaries may not be bypassed.
- Escrow is reversible until sale. Store equipment remains owned. A completed sale must remove the item and preserve enough identity for its authored callback to matter later.
- Living situations must never borrow RNG rarity language. Emergence is deterministic and causality receipts declare whether randomness participated.
- Ignoring a situation is world state, not a paused objective. The situation must continue with an authored consequence.
- Every claim of visual quality requires captured frames at representative sizes plus interaction evidence.
- Generated assets require provenance. No franchise imitation and no unlicensed source material.
- Never call the whole game “finished” from a vertical-slice test. Expansion gates remain explicit.
