# Known limits - Recurring Commons Service milestone

- This is a browser-based pre-alpha, not a completed "best game on Earth" claim.
- Six locations, one four-resident district, two persistent neighborhood households, one bounded employer/competitor, one deep storefront, one family household, the first home/portal tiers, the first Starspite deck, and six persistent Life Thread families are implemented. The intended planetary world is much bigger.
- Runtime character art is procedural 2D Canvas/DOM animation anchored by one authored key-art image; production character rigs, performance capture, and a full 3D asset library do not yet exist.
- Audio is procedural Web Audio after player interaction. It is not a finished score, voice cast, or mastered soundscape.
- Economy reactions are deterministic browser-local simulation, not a server-hosted shared economy.
- Alien-web buyers remain five authored profiles. Escrow, deterministic offers/counters, daily income, callbacks, identities/branches, upgrades, equipment, one pet shift, one supplier factor, one paid-production partner, household returns, and one recurring public service are playable; planetary suppliers, broad competitors, autonomous employees, manufacturing, taxation, bulk logistics, and longitudinal balance remain absent.
- Lopsided Lane has four residents, four base phases, supplier/household/maintenance override layers, four repeatable resident arcs, one three-order work calendar plus one declared due-state override, object-state reactions, gifts, deliveries, and recurring service marks. This is a local dependency graph, not a citywide population simulation or general civic economy.
- Crooked Kettle has four standing bands and exact business/schedule consequences. Hushglass has four warmth bands plus four maintenance-integrity bands, a four-day service calendar, three lived-state routes, exact returns/overdue recovery, and visible aftermath. Two households and one service loop do not prove autonomous household negotiation or a planetary supplier network.
- Long Table Works remains one employer/competitor with three rotating base orders, one Hushglass due-state override, two approaches, three pressure bands, literal pet labor, owned-tool wear, and exact returns. It is not a broad labor market or employee-management system.
- Living Threads now provide six situations and 31 authored approaches, including resident, employment, supplier, object, and literal-pet routes. They prove deterministic emergence and exact consequences for the current catalog, not a planet-scale social graph or large narrative library.
- Saves use browser localStorage plus manual export/import. Cloud sync and cross-device recovery are absent.
- The first Starspite deck, three games, authored market, item insurance, receipts, Vesper, and probability-crime activity are playable. Broader ship districts, social games, bidder simulations, alternate admission, and casino-business ownership are not built.
- Casino and portal receipts prove this implementation's selected seed, mapping, fixed odds, and replay. They do not make software RNG a physical entropy oracle or prove future builds unchanged.
- Accessibility includes keyboard routes, reduced-motion handling, visible focus, text alternatives, semantic names, modal scroll locking, and a responsive 390x844 audit. Full screen-reader, physical assistive-technology, and diverse-human audits remain open.
- Live verification promoted bounded screenshots and proved two sampled district frames changed over 650 ms. No rolling-frame capture was available; continuous animation timing and frame pacing remain UNKNOWN.
- Long-duration balance, localization, independent playtesting, production performance budgets, platform certification, and external comparative quality evidence remain open.
