# Known limits - Neighborhood Debt Aftermath + hybrid low-poly 3D

- Status is **WORKING**, not CANON and not a completed “best game on Earth” claim.
- Six places, four Lane residents, two persistent neighborhood households, one bounded employer/competitor, one deep storefront, one family household, the first home/portal tiers, the first Starspite deck, six Life Thread families, one recurring service loop, and three debt-aftermath routes are implemented. The intended planetary world is much bigger.
- The new 3D layer is dependency-free low-poly WebGL with cube/pyramid forms, one block-built Pip, authored architecture, and 16-step lighting. It is not a production model, rig, animation, LOD, VFX, cinematic, or performance pipeline. The procedural Canvas layer remains interaction authority and fallback.
- The live pass proved WebGL compilation and six distinct scene routes on one 1280x720 browser/GPU path. WebGL context loss, broad GPU compatibility, compact viewports, physical devices, continuous frame pacing, and production performance budgets remain unverified.
- Audio is procedural Web Audio after player interaction. It is not a finished score, voice cast, or mastered soundscape.
- Economy reactions are deterministic browser-local simulation, not a server-hosted shared economy.
- The three recovery routes can recur across distinct saved losses and leave distinct marks, but only one live Long Table cycle was observed. Multi-cycle balance, abuse resistance across long sessions, and independent player comprehension remain unproven.
- Recovery restores at most the named loss and remains separate from Starspite net/storefront income. It is an authored life-sim response, not financial advice, a real-money system, or a general debt simulation.
- Alien-web buyers remain five authored profiles. Planetary suppliers, broad competitors, autonomous employees, manufacturing, taxation, bulk logistics, and longitudinal balance remain absent.
- Lopsided Lane is a local dependency graph, not a citywide population simulation or general civic economy. Crooked Kettle, Hushglass, and Long Table do not prove autonomous institution negotiation.
- Living Threads provide six situations and 31 authored approaches. They prove deterministic emergence and exact consequences for the current catalog, not a planet-scale social graph or large narrative library.
- Saves use browser localStorage plus manual export/import. Cloud/Steam Cloud integration, cross-device recovery, account migration, and save-conflict resolution are absent.
- The first Starspite deck has three games, one authored market, insurance, receipts, Vesper, and probability-crime activity. Broader ship districts, social games, bidder simulations, alternate admission, and ownership are not built.
- Casino and portal receipts prove this implementation's selected seed, mapping, fixed odds, and replay. They do not make software RNG a physical entropy oracle or prove future builds unchanged.
- Accessibility includes keyboard routes, reduced-motion handling, visible focus, semantic names, modal scroll locking, and prior compact evidence. The new debt panel received desktop semantic/geometry checks only; fresh compact, screen-reader, physical assistive-technology, and diverse-human audits remain open.
- Steam build/depot configuration, controller/gamepad support, overlay behavior, achievements, store assets/copy, legal/privacy review, platform QA, install/update/uninstall checks, and release authority remain outside this pass.
- Long-duration balance, localization, independent playtesting, platform certification, and external comparative-quality evidence remain open.
