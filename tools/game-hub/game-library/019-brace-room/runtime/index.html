How to brace

  • Run to a station showing an amber ring and hold/tap your action key.
  • Engine Bay & Vent — mash your action key.
  • Breaker & Ballast — hold your action key steady.
  • Radio — a dot orbits the station once per beat; tap your action key right when it flashes bright.
  • Bulkhead — needs two crew holding together at once. Cannot be soloed.
  • A grey ring is a false alarm — leave it alone, don't act on it.
  • Let a real fault run out, or act on a false one, and the hull takes damage.
