THE BREACH

Attackers march straight to their side. A fallen tower opens that half. Inside, enemies spread over three paths and their damage doubles. The bright rebuild timer gives a cheaper, stronger reconstruction.

BUILDING PLAN

Each side owns four plots and a separate wallet. Forge makes your metal. Moon Market makes your gold. Ballista Yard attacks your lane. Ember Lab splash-burns breaches on your side. Build cards show exact current and next-level output.

THE FIVE OATHS

Every 72 seconds a named siege changes the enemy mix and opens with a formation. Hexers ward nearby ordinary troops, so break their teal rings first. Warlords accelerate nearby troops, so split the red command ring. Each sealed oath pays both active wardens; after oath five, endless scaling continues.
