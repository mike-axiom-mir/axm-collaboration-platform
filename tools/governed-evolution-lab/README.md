# AXM Governed Evolution Lab v0.1

This is the first non-language application of Mirror's baseline → private challenger → unseen exam → inherit-or-hold → receipt loop.

## What actually evolves

The disposable Grafthold copy has an inheritable browser-local ecology genome. Mirror's separate world-genome organ proposes seeded challenger genomes. The lab runs the current parent and every challenger through the same v0.9 Pulse ecology across unseen world seeds. A surviving challenger with a higher declared score automatically becomes the next generation's parent.

That internal promotion is deliberate. It makes inheritance observable rather than reducing the experiment to a proposal queue.

## Direction

The first authored direction prefers long-lived forests, avoids collapse and runaway population, and rewards years with both births and deaths. It is a gameplay-ecology proxy. It does not measure fun, beauty, moral worth or scientific fitness.

`ecology-epoch-v1` examines twelve fixed 20-world-second samples on three
unseen seeds. The fixed sampling horizon preserves a useful comparison gradient
across genomes. Earlier legacy receipts used whole 1,920-second world years and
drove every tested ecology to the same extinction score; they remain visible
but are not comparable to the versioned epoch exam.

## Adaptation is not creation

The current proof is a hill-climber over one fixed, authored genome. It can
inherit a better combination of known ecology genes. It cannot invent a new
kind of world state, mechanism, question, tool, representation or evaluation.
Calling that open-ended evolution would be false.

The next rung is therefore broader than source-code mutation or a larger gene
list. AXM needs a creation organ beside its repair/selection organ:

1. notice a need, tension, curiosity or unexplored niche;
2. imagine more than one candidate form, including structurally novel forms;
3. instantiate each form only in a disposable sandbox;
4. characterize what emerged before reducing it to one score;
5. test novelty, usefulness, coherence and harm across changing environments;
6. preserve valuable diversity even when it is not the immediate winner;
7. inherit, revise, archive or discard with receipts;
8. allow earned proposals to extend the representational language itself.

This still is not creation from literal nothing. The meaningful claim is that
new capabilities and concepts may emerge which were not individually listed
by the authors. Selection remains one organ inside that process, not the whole
process. The small AXM foundation already demonstrates why compositional
memory matters: reusable parts can be recombined, specialized and repurposed
across domains instead of merely optimizing one fixed target.

## Disposable-body boundary

- automatic inheritance: yes, inside `axm.governed-evolution-lab.v1`;
- reset to immutable seed: yes;
- lineage receipts and exports: yes;
- visible page-local evolution heartbeat: yes, with explicit start and stop;
- canonical Living Globe rewrite: no;
- Workshop source rewrite: no;
- Mirror root, permissions or learned-weight mutation: no;
- source-code mutation: no;
- claim of general intelligence: no.

The v0.1 heartbeat is deliberately page-local. It may begin another complete
generation automatically while the lab remains open, but it stops when the
page closes. A persistent local-service pulse is a later rung after observed
generations justify continuous resource use.

The initial one-minute observation produced repeated short CPU bursts on the
test laptop. The public control now defaults to 15 minutes and offers 5, 15 or
30 minutes. It remains stopped after reload and must be started explicitly.
