Independent build seats

Every participant receives the same locked challenge before cross-review. Revisions and losing paths remain preserved.

Evidence before opinion

Thirty contracts, twenty-eight deterministic validator kinds, reproducible evidence bundles, and integrity verification support—but do not replace—judgment.

Assigned blind review

No self-voting or unassigned review. Scores bind to evidence or become explicit abstentions; ties and dissent remain visible.

Authority boundary

Results and merge maps are proposals. Only Mike can accept, merge, branch, rerun, reject, hold, install, promote, or canonize them.
