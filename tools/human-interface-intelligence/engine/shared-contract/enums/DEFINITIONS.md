# Enumerations and Definitions

## Information state

- `KNOWN`: directly supported by a cited source.
- `INFERRED`: reasoned from cited facts; reasoning and source basis are mandatory.
- `UNKNOWN`: evidence is absent or insufficient.
- `CONFLICTED`: credible sources disagree.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: the field does not apply to this capability.

## Beginner operation class

- `simplified_control`: same operation, fewer unnecessary decisions.
- `restricted_safe_operation`: a safer subset of the capability.
- `guided_advanced_operation`: advanced operation exposed through staged guidance and checks.
- `full_expert_control`: complete declared control; not a beginner substitute.
