# Shared Terminology

**Capability declaration** — original machine-facing statement of what exists.

**Capability record** — shared, human-readable classification linked to the declaration.

**Interface pattern** — reusable interaction structure, not a capability-specific mock screen.

**Recommendation context** — user, goal, device, tools, accessibility, privacy, and budget constraints used for one recommendation.

**Beginner layer** — a declared safe/simplified operating mode; never a claim of full capability coverage.

**Capability cost** — compute, time, attention, skill, setup, recovery, and interaction burden.

**Proof status** — evidence level of the capability itself, not visual polish of an interface.

**Conditional recommendation** — usable only with stated gates, tools, knowledge, or safeguards.

**No safe match** — evidence or risk prevents honest exposure through the available patterns.
